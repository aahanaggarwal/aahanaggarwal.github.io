Physics model and proofs

A closed 2D cell model with finite molecular inventories, pressure-dependent water phases, partial vaporization, oxygen-limited chemistry, heat conduction, gravity, gas diffusion, and a coupled liquid/gas pressure and viscosity solve. Communicating vessels level through pressure; mineral mixtures retain separate fusion plateaus. Lean defines the entire timestep and proves conservation of all 11 tracked elements and total energy. Rust hosts the generated program in WebAssembly.

The proofs establish properties of this declared model. Coarse cell mechanics, rounded properties and simplified reaction kinetics remain approximations. Painting adds or removes matter and energy; conservation applies between edits.
